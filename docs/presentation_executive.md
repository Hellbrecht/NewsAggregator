# Water Risk Intelligence Monitor (Executive - 5 Slides)

## Slide 1 - Mission

**Water Risk Intelligence Monitor**  
One workspace to monitor water-risk news, market movement, and hydrology conditions across North America.

---

## Slide 2 - The Problem

- Water risk signals are fragmented across separate systems.
- News, stock reactions, and hydrology data are rarely connected in real time.
- Teams lose time validating risk and deciding what is actionable.

---

## Slide 3 - The Product

- `News`: aggregated and risk-filtered coverage
- `Stocks`: water-linked watchlist with quote refresh and ticker drill-through
- `Hydrology Live`: public hydrological monitoring modules
- `Alerts`: prioritized high-risk events for fast triage

---

## Slide 4 - Current Value

- Faster detection of emerging water-related risk
- Better confidence from multi-signal confirmation
- Cleaner operator workflow in a single dashboard
- Config-driven architecture for source expansion

---

## Slide 5 - Next Steps

- Expand regional data/source coverage
- Improve cross-tab event correlation
- Add threshold-based operator notifications
- Strengthen decision support for operations and risk teams
