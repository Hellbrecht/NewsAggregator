# Water Risk Intelligence Monitor

## Slide 1 - Title

**Water Risk Intelligence Monitor**  
Aggregating water-risk news, market signals, and hydrology data across North America.

---

## Slide 2 - The Problem

- Water-risk signals are scattered across many platforms.
- News, market reactions, and physical water indicators are rarely viewed together.
- Teams lose time validating whether a risk signal is real, local, and urgent.

---

## Slide 3 - Our Solution

- One operational workspace with four views:
- `News` for risk-related coverage
- `Stocks` for water-linked watchlist tracking
- `Hydrology Live` for public hydrological indicators
- `Alerts` for prioritized high-risk events

---

## Slide 4 - News Intelligence

- Aggregates configured sources on a schedule.
- Supports manual scan for immediate updates.
- Classifies and filters entries by water relevance and risk signal.
- Deduplicates repeated stories.

---

## Slide 5 - Stock Intelligence

- Tracks water-linked equities in a watchlist.
- Refreshes quotes on a fixed cadence (5 minutes).
- Handles provider fallback when data is unavailable.
- Ticker links open directly on Yahoo Finance for deeper analysis.

---

## Slide 6 - Hydrology Intelligence

- Monitors public hydrological data modules in North America.
- Keeps modules in consistent card layouts for operator readability.
- Supports in-card scrolling for dense datasets while preserving visual structure.
- Enables rapid cross-check between narrative risk and physical measurements.

---

## Slide 7 - Alerting & Prioritization

- Highlights high-priority events by risk score.
- Supports filtering by time window, risk threshold, and type.
- Designed to reduce noise and focus attention on action-worthy events.

---

## Slide 8 - Why It Matters

- Earlier detection of water-related operational and financial risk.
- Better confidence through multi-signal confirmation.
- Faster triage and response with a single unified interface.

---

## Slide 9 - Typical Users

- Infrastructure and utility operations teams
- Risk and resilience analysts
- Public sector monitoring groups
- Investors following water-related sectors

---

## Slide 10 - Current Status & Next Steps
